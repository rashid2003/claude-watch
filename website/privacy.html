privacy

the short version: we can't read your stuff.

Updated October 2026. This covers the Mac app, the iphone app, the relay, and this website.

▸ the mac app

Reads Claude's transcript and config files on your own disk to show you what your sessions are doing. It does not send your conversations, code, usage data or anything else to us or to any third party. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporter phoning home.

▸ the iphone connection

Your phone and Mac talk over an end-to-end encrypted channel (X25519 key agreement, ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption). The key is generated on your Mac and transferred to the phone inside the pairing QR code — it never touches a server. When the relay is used, it forwards opaque ciphertext and keeps no message content. You can bypass the relay entirely with direct Wi-Fi or your own tailnet.

▸ push notifications

Notification payloads are delivered through Apple's push service. They carry the minimum needed to show the alert, and sensitive detail stays on your devices.

▸ purchases

Payments are processed by Stripe; your card never touches our systems. For an active license we store: your email address, the plan, and its status. That's the entire customer database. Ask us to delete it anytime at hello@lajward.dev.

▸ this website

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers. The pricing page talks to our billing service only when you apply a code or start a checkout.

▸ app store label

The iphone app's privacy label is “Data Not Collected”, and it means it.
