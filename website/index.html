private by design

your code never leaves your machines.

On the Mac, everything is read from local files — nothing is scraped from windows, nothing is sent anywhere. The phone connects through an end-to-end encrypted relay: the key is pinned in the pairing QR, so the relay only ever sees ciphertext. Both sides dial out, which means no open ports, no VPN, no Tailscale required.

e2ex25519 + chacha20-poly1305, key pinned at pairing

faq

questions people actually ask.

what exactly does it watch?

The Claude desktop app (every profile you run, including extra accounts and orgs) and Claude Code running in any terminal. Session Watch reads the transcript files Claude writes locally — it never scrapes your screen and never injects anything you didn't ask for.

does my code or conversation go through your servers?

No. On the Mac, everything is read from local files. The phone connection is end-to-end encrypted — the encryption key is created on your Mac and pinned into the pairing QR code, so the relay forwards ciphertext it cannot read. If you prefer, switch to direct Wi-Fi or your own tailnet and skip the relay entirely.

what happens when my mac sleeps or goes offline?

The iphone app says so — the live activity and widgets show mac offline and asleep states, and your cached chats stay readable. When the Mac is back, everything picks up where it left off.

how does auto-retry work?

When an account hits its usage limit, Session Watch forecasts the reset time and queues a retry. The moment the window resets, it sends “continue” — through the desktop app or the CLI, your choice per account — and verifies against the transcript that the run actually resumed.

why isn't the mac app in the mac app store?

The App Store requires the sandbox, and the sandbox forbids the Accessibility and Automation permissions that let Session Watch type replies and answer prompts for you. So it ships as a signed, notarized download instead — Gatekeeper checks it like any other developer-signed app.
